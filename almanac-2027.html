The British Green Witch's Almanac 2027

Moon Phases, Sabbats, Hedgerow Lore & Seasonal Magic for the Year Ahead in the British Isles

Your year of moons, festivals and hedgerow magic, made for these islands.

The British Green Witch's Almanac 2027 follows the year as it really unfolds in Britain and Ireland: our own light, our own hedges, our own bank holidays and village customs. Every date and time has been calculated or checked for 2027, so you can plan your rituals, walks and celebrations with confidence.

Inside the book

  • Every new and full moon in GMT and BST, plus sunrise and sunset for London, Cardiff, York, Edinburgh and Belfast
  • The eight festivals of the Wheel of the Year, with their 2027 dates and exact astronomical moments
  • The partial solar eclipse of 2 August 2027: times across the UK and how to watch it safely
  • Genuine British folk customs with their 2027 dates, from the Haxey Hood and Up Helly Aa to Helston Flora Day, Punkie Night and the Stonehaven fireballs
  • The year's meteor showers with 2027 moonlight conditions, and the naked-eye planets month by month, calculated for the UK
  • Moon-gardening dates for every month, clearly labelled as traditional lore
  • Wildlife to watch for, from bluebells, swifts and glow-worms to red deer, seal pups and starling murmurations
  • A calendar page and a Week by Week journal page for every month, plus free A4 printables on this site

Honest about what is ancient and what is modern, and free of American dates and recipes, this is a green witch's year, the British way. A companion to The British Green Witch.

By Mark Hutchinson. Part of the Modern Witchcraft & Wicca series.
