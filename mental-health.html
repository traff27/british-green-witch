Witchcraft & Mental Health

Nature's Magic

Gentle, nature-based practices for calm, connection and meaning, with honest advice on what they can and can't do.

Witchcraft & Mental Health: Nature's Magic brings together the green witch's craft and responsible, up-to-date UK wellbeing guidance. It shows how herbs, ritual, the seasons and the Moon can bring structure, comfort and beauty to your days, while being clear that they work alongside your GP and NHS care, never instead of it.

Inside the book

  • Herbal allies including lavender, chamomile, lemon balm, peppermint, valerian, rosemary and rose, with tradition and evidence kept clearly apart and full safety notes
  • Daily practices: mornings, evenings, sleep, breathing, mindfulness and grounding
  • Rituals and spells for protection, self-love and renewal, with smoke-free cleansing and safe candle practice
  • What the research says about time outdoors, green social prescribing, expressive writing and gratitude journals
  • A full chapter on where to get help

This book is about self-care and wellbeing. It is not a substitute for professional medical or mental health care. If you are struggling, please speak to your GP or doctor.

By Mark Hutchinson. Part of the Modern Witchcraft & Wicca series.
