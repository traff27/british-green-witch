The British Green Witch

A Hedgerow Year of Herbs, Folklore & Seasonal Magic for the British Isles

Step outside: the year is already turning.

The British Green Witch follows the hedgerow month by month, from the first gorse flowers of January to the evergreens of midwinter. It shows you how to know, gather and celebrate the plants on your doorstep, safely, legally and kindly.

Inside the book

  • Twelve monthly chapters with plant profiles, look-alike warnings and a danger box for every month
  • Seasonal recipes, rituals, crafts and old customs from Cornwall to Moray, Wales and Ireland
  • The Wheel of the Year as it is kept in Britain, with folklore that is honest about what is old and what is new
  • A foraging calendar, a danger and look-alike quick reference, and a plain-English guide to the law
  • Sixteen botanical plates and sources listed for every chapter

Grounded in guidance from the Woodland Trust, the RHS, the BSBI, the NHS and the law itself, this is a green witch's companion for the hedges, woods and gardens of the British Isles.

By Mark Hutchinson. Part of the Modern Witchcraft & Wicca series.
