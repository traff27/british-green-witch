The Green Witch's Encyclopedia

Herbs, Magic & Healing

Sixty-six herbs. Their lore, their magic, and how to use them safely.

The Green Witch's Encyclopedia is a working reference for anyone drawn to the old craft of herbs. Each plant is set out the same way: how to recognise it, where its folklore comes from, how green witches use it today, and what you need to know before it goes anywhere near a cup, a bath or a candle.

Inside the book

  • Sixty-six herb profiles: fifty from agrimony to yarrow, plus sixteen wild herbs of the British hedgerow, meadow and wood, with traditional names, history, magical associations and clear safety notes
  • A full table of correspondences, plus simple spells, teas, sachets, incense and bath blends
  • Growing, gathering, drying and storing your herbs, with the forager's rules and a picking calendar
  • The Wheel of the Year and gentle green-witch practices for everyday wellbeing
  • A safety quick reference, forty botanical plates, a glossary and sources for every chapter

Honest about what is folklore and what is evidence, and clear that herbs are no substitute for your GP, this is a green witch's companion you will reach for again and again.

By Mark Hutchinson. Part of the Modern Witchcraft & Wicca series.
