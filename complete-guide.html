Modern Witchcraft & Wicca

The Complete Guide To Modern Witchcraft & Wicca

The Enchanted Path

Where witchcraft came from, what Wiccans believe, and how to begin your own practice.

The Complete Guide To Modern Witchcraft & Wicca is an honest, practical introduction to the Craft. It tells the real story of witchcraft and Wicca, from the cunning folk and the witch trials to Gerald Gardner, Doreen Valiente and the Pagans of today, and then shows you, step by step, how to practise safely, ethically and in your own way.

Inside the book

  • The history of witchcraft and the birth of Wicca, with myth separated from evidence
  • The gods and goddesses, the Wheel of the Year and the eight sabbats
  • The witch's tools, casting a circle, spellcraft and the ethics of magic
  • Nature, the elements and a starter herbal grimoire with clear safety notes
  • Covens, communities and staying safe in groups, plus twelve rituals and spells to begin with
  • A reader's guide to the books that shaped modern witchcraft, from Leland and Graves to Gardner, Valiente, Buckland, Starhawk and Hutton

With sources for every chapter, a glossary, botanical plates and practical advice on UK law, safety and wellbeing, this is a guide to walking the enchanted path with open eyes.

By Mark Hutchinson. Part of the Modern Witchcraft & Wicca series.
