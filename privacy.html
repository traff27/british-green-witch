Privacy

This site is straightforward on purpose.

  • It does not use analytics or advertising of its own, and it does not set any tracking of its own in your browser.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site. The printables page loads a sign-up form from MailerLite, who may set cookies in order to run the form. Other pages have no third-party embeds.
  • To get the free printables, you sign up with your email address. Emails are collected via MailerLite (our processor) so we can send you the printables and occasional news about the books. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. If you have a question or want to be removed, write to hello@britishgreenwitch.co.uk.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub will receive the usual server logs that come with visiting any website, such as your IP address and the pages you request, under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

If you email hello@britishgreenwitch.co.uk, we will use your message and address only to reply to you. We will not add you to the mailing list from a personal email, and we will not sell or share your address.
