Privacy

This site is straightforward on purpose.

  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.
  • It does not set any tracking in your browser.
  • Fonts, images and scripts are all served from this site. There are no third-party embeds.
  • The printables are direct PDF downloads. No email address is required, and none is collected on that page at present.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub will receive the usual server logs that come with visiting any website, such as your IP address and the pages you request, under GitHub’s own privacy policy.

If you email hello@britishgreenwitch.co.uk, we will use your message and address only to reply to you. We will not add you to a mailing list from a personal email, and we will not sell or share your address.

A mailing-list form is planned for the printables page later. When it is added, this privacy page will be updated first, and you will be asked to opt in. Signing up will never be required to download the printables.
