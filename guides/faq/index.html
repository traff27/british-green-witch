Practice

Do I need to be Wiccan to be a green witch?

No. Wicca is a specific modern religion that became public in England in the 1950s. Green witchcraft is a nature-based practice that many people follow without any religion, and some combine with Wicca or other paths. See what “green witch” means.

Is the Wheel of the Year ancient?

The festivals are old, but the wheel is modern. Samhain, Imbolc, Beltane and Lughnasadh are named in early medieval Irish writing, and midsummer, midwinter and Lammas were kept in Britain for centuries. Combining eight festivals into one evenly spaced cycle dates from English modern Paganism in the 1950s and 1960s. More on the Wheel of the Year page.

Are the dates on this site in British time?

Yes. Every date and time is given in UK clock time, GMT in winter and BST in summer, and is checked against published astronomical sources. See the Wheel of the Year 2026 and 2027 and the 2027 UK moon calendar.

Is Yule 2027 on 21 or 22 December?

In the UK the winter solstice falls at 02:42 GMT on Wednesday 22 December 2027, so the longest night is Tuesday 21 to Wednesday 22 December. American websites often say 21 December because, in US time zones, the solstice is still the evening of the 21st.

Safety and the law

Is it legal to forage in the UK?

Picking small amounts of wild fruit, flowers, leaves and fungi for your own use is generally lawful, but in Great Britain it is an offence to uproot any wild plant without the landowner’s permission, some species are fully protected, and many reserves ban picking. Scotland has its own Outdoor Access Code. This is a summary, not legal advice; see foraging and the law in brief.

Can I use herbs instead of medicine?

No. Our books describe folklore and traditional uses and are clear that herbs are no substitute for your GP or pharmacist. Many herbs interact with medicines or are unsafe in pregnancy, so always ask a pharmacist or doctor before taking any herb in more than food amounts.

Is it safe to burn herbs, incense or candles at home?

It can be, with care. Never leave a flame unattended, use a heat-proof holder away from curtains and pets, keep the room ventilated, and avoid smoke if anyone in the house has asthma or breathing problems. Never burn a plant you have not identified with certainty.

What should I do if someone eats a plant that might be poisonous?

Call NHS 111 for advice, or 999 if the person is seriously unwell, drowsy, having a fit or struggling to breathe. Take the plant, or a clear photo of it, with you.
